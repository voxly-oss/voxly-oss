# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 28, 2026**

Voxly ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Voxly platform and voxly.dev website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Full name
- Agency name
- Phone number (optional)

### API Keys (BYOK)
If you use Bring Your Own Key (BYOK), your API keys are encrypted at rest and only used to make requests on your behalf. We never share your keys with third parties.

### Usage Data
We collect anonymized usage data including:
- Pages visited
- Features used
- Error logs (via Sentry)

### GitHub Data
When you connect your GitHub account, we access:
- Repository names and metadata
- Commit messages and PR titles
- Build/workflow status

We do **not** access your source code unless explicitly authorized.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Service delivery**: To operate and maintain your Voxly instance
- **Communication**: To send notifications via your configured channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Email)
- **Improvement**: To improve the platform and fix bugs
- **Support**: To respond to your requests

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share data only with:
- **AI Providers** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google): Your queries are sent to the AI provider you configure. We do not store AI conversation logs beyond your session.
- **Twilio**: Phone numbers for WhatsApp delivery.
- **Payment Processors**: Stripe or Razorpay for billing.

## Data Security

- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt
- API keys are encrypted at rest
- All API endpoints require JWT authentication
- CORS is restricted to authorized origins
- Rate limiting protects against brute-force attacks

## Data Retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active
- You can request deletion at any time by emailing **privacy@voxly.dev**
- We delete your data within 30 days of a deletion request

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data
- Withdraw consent

## Cookies

We use essential cookies only for authentication (JWT tokens). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

## Children

Voxly is not intended for users under 16 years of age.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification.

## Contact

For privacy questions: **privacy@voxly.dev**
